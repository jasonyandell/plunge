About this table

Play phase only. Each deal is random; a fixed rule stands in for the auction: the side with more high-card points declares, in an 8+ card major fit, else no-trump with 25+ points, else an 8+ card minor fit, else no-trump, at a level set by the combined points. The seats are turned so that you always sit South. Making the contract is all that counts (no scoring of overtricks).

Walt decides each card by sampling the deals it cannot rule out (respecting the dummy, the cards played and the voids shown) and choosing one card across all of them: the one that makes (or defeats) the contract in the most sampled deals. It models every other seat as a simpler player deciding from its own chair, for the next couple of tricks, and lets a cheap rule-based player finish each sampled deal. Declarer's Walt decides for dummy too, reading declarer's hand, dummy and the table only.

Exploratory experiment. Report: lab/bridge/REPORT.md in the Plunge repository.
