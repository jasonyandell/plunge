Rules used and how Walt plays

Suit games: the four jacks (♣J ♠J ♥J ♦J) then A 10 K Q 9 8 7 of the trump suit are trumps. Grand: only jacks are trumps. Follow the led suit (jacks belong to the trump suit); if you can't, play anything. A 11, 10 10, K 4, Q 3, J 2. The declarer's 2 discarded skat cards count for the declarer at the end.

No auction: the strongest hand declares, picks up the skat, and chooses its game. Forehand (the seat after the dealer) leads trick 1. Schneider, schwarz, null and game value are ignored — it's win (61+) or lose.

Walt decides by sampling the deals it cannot rule out from its own chair (respecting hand sizes and revealed voids; the skat is unknown to defenders), choosing one card across all of them, and modelling each other seat as a level-0 player deciding from its own chair. It searches a few plies with those minds, then plays out with a simple lawful rule policy; in the last tricks it searches to the end. It never looks at your cards.
