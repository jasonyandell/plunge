Hearts you (South) vs three Walt players

About this experiment

Rules: 4 players, no partners, 13 tricks. 2♣ leads trick one; follow suit if you can; no hearts or Q♠ on trick one unless you hold nothing else; hearts can't be led until a heart has been played (unless you hold only hearts). Each heart is 1 point, Q♠ is 13. Take all 26 and you shoot the moon: you score 0, everyone else 26. Passing rotates left, right, across, hold. A hand ends once every point card has been taken. Game ends when someone reaches 100; lowest total wins.

Walt (EXPLORATORY): each Walt seat samples deals consistent with its own hand, the public record and the cards it passed, then picks one card for all of them, modeling the other seats as players who answer the same question from their own chairs one rung lower (bottom rung: random legal). Objective: minimize its own hand score, summed over sampled deals (exact integers). Modeled minds look a few plays ahead; beyond that a quick rule-based playout finishes the hand. Walt seats pass with a fixed heuristic, not by search.
